Standard tickets start from €49. Premium seats from €129, VIP boxes from €399, and Executive Boxes from €1,499. Champions League matches are priced higher due to demand.

The most vocal supporters are typically in the home end/ultras section. For the best overall view, choose the main stand or premium midfield seats.

Yes, we have tickets for Manchester City away matches across all competitions. Away section tickets are limited and sell out quickly - book early.
