Frequently Asked Questions

**How much are Real Madrid tickets?**
Standard tickets start from €49. Premium seats from €129, VIP boxes from €399, and Executive Boxes from €1,499. Champions League matches are priced higher due to demand.

**How early should I arrive at Santiago Bernabeu?**
We recommend arriving at least 90 minutes before kick-off. For big matches (El Clasico derbies, Champions League), arrive 2 hours early to enjoy the pre-match atmosphere.

**Can I buy tickets for away matches?**
Yes, we have tickets for Real Madrid away matches across all competitions. Away section tickets are limited and sell out quickly - book early.

**What matches can I see at Santiago Bernabeu?**
Real Madrid plays approximately 25 home matches per season across La Liga, Champions League, and domestic cup competitions. Derby matches and Champions League knockout rounds are the highest demand.

**Is Santiago Bernabeu accessible for disabled fans?**
Yes, Santiago Bernabeu offers wheelchair-accessible seating, accessible toilets, and dedicated assistance. Contact us when booking to arrange accessible tickets.
