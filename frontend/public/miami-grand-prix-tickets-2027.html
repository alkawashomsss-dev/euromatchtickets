What Makes Miami Grand Prix Unmissable

The Miami Grand Prix is a 57-lap battle on the 5.412km Miami International Autodrome. With a capacity of 80,000 fans, this daytime race with clear visibility of every overtaking move in Miami delivers non-stop action. Watch Lando Norris (McLaren) and Oscar Piastri (Aston Martin) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Miami

The Beach Club area is the place to be seen, but the Turn 7 grandstand offers the best racing action. Miami's heat is intense - bring sunscreen, a hat, and stay hydrated. The campus area has live DJs and entertainment throughout the weekend. Book a hotel in South Beach for the ultimate Miami GP experience.

#

How to Get There

Miami International Airport is 30 minutes away. Uber and Lyft work well. Dedicated shuttles run from Aventura Mall and other pickup points.
