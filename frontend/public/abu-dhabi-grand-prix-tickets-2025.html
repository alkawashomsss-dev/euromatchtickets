What Makes Abu Dhabi Grand Prix Unmissable

The Abu Dhabi Grand Prix is a 58-lap battle on the 5.281km Yas Marina Circuit. With a capacity of 60,000 fans, this twilight race that starts in daylight and finishes under lights in Abu Dhabi delivers non-stop action. Watch Max Verstappen (Aston Martin) and George Russell (Alpine) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Abu Dhabi

The Yas Hotel bridge is the most iconic photo opportunity in F1. Post-race concerts have featured Eminem, The Weeknd, and Swedish House Mafia. Yas Waterworld and Ferrari World are minutes from the circuit for pre-race entertainment. The North Grandstand offers the best view of the hotel bridge. Book a room at the W Abu Dhabi for the ultimate experience.

#

How to Get There

Abu Dhabi International Airport is 15 minutes from Yas Island. Free shuttle buses operate from Abu Dhabi city centre. Many hotels on Yas Island are walking distance from the circuit.
