What Makes Italian Grand Prix Unmissable

The Italian Grand Prix is a 53-lap battle on the 5.793km Autodromo Nazionale Monza. With a capacity of 113,000 fans, this daytime race with clear visibility of every overtaking move in Monza delivers non-stop action. Watch Charles Leclerc (McLaren) and George Russell (Ferrari) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Monza

The best view is at the Variante della Roggia chicane (Turn 4-5) where heavy braking creates overtaking. Experience the podium ceremony from the main straight - it's unforgettable when Ferrari wins. Combine with a trip to Milan for fashion, art, and the Duomo. Arrive early on Sunday - the atmosphere builds from dawn as tifosi gather.

#

How to Get There

Milan Malpensa Airport is 60 minutes away. Milan Linate is closer at 30 minutes. Direct trains from Milano Centrale to Monza, then shuttle to the circuit. A car-free day in Monza park makes the walk scenic.
