What Makes Emilia Romagna Grand Prix Unmissable

The Emilia Romagna Grand Prix is a 63-lap battle on the 4.909km Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari. Insider Tips for Imola

Stay in nearby Bologna for incredible food - it's the gastronomic capital of Italy. The Rivazza grandstand offers views of cars navigating the downhill section. Bring earplugs for the main straight. Visit the Senna memorial at Tamburello corner - a pilgrimage for any F1 fan.
