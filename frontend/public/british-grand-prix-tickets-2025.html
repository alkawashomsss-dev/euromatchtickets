Insider Tips for Silverstone

Pack a raincoat - British weather is unpredictable even in July. The Copse corner grandstand is superb for seeing cars at maximum speed. The general admission areas are vast and well-positioned. The Silverstone village has excellent pubs for post-race celebrations. Arrive on Friday for the full festival experience.

How to Get There

Birmingham Airport is 50 minutes away. Shuttle buses from Milton Keynes and Northampton run frequently. The A43 and M1 motorway provide direct access, but expect significant race day traffic.
