What Makes Las Vegas Grand Prix Unmissable

The Las Vegas Grand Prix is a 50-lap battle on the 6.201km Las Vegas Strip Circuit. With a capacity of 105,000 fans, this spectacular night race under floodlights in Las Vegas delivers non-stop action. Watch Max Verstappen (Aston Martin) and Lando Norris (Red Bull Racing) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Las Vegas

The Bellagio fountain grandstand is the most Instagram-worthy seat in F1. November nights in Vegas are cold (5-10C) - bring warm clothing despite the desert location. The Sphere is visible from multiple grandstands and displays F1 graphics during the race. Book a Strip-view hotel room for an incredible pre-race atmosphere. The after-parties in Vegas clubs are legendary.

#

How to Get There

Harry Reid International Airport is 10 minutes from the Strip. Monorail and rideshare are the best circuit transport options. Most grandstands are walking distance from major hotels.
