What Makes Singapore Grand Prix Unmissable

The Singapore Grand Prix is a 62-lap battle on the 5.063km Marina Bay Street Circuit. With a capacity of 85,000 fans, this spectacular night race under floodlights in Singapore delivers non-stop action. Watch Carlos Sainz (Mercedes) and Fernando Alonso (Red Bull Racing) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Singapore

The Padang grandstand offers a view of cars racing past colonial architecture - a photographer's dream. Singapore's hawker centres serve incredible food for under 5 dollars - visit Maxwell or Lau Pa Sat. The post-race concerts feature A-list performers (past acts include Beyonce, The Killers, Green Day). Start hydrating days before the race - the humidity is intense.

#

How to Get There

Changi Airport is connected by MRT (metro) in 30 minutes. The City Hall and Esplanade MRT stations are the closest to the circuit. Singapore's public transport is world-class.
