The Dutch Grand Prix is a 72-lap battle on the 4.259km Circuit Zandvoort. With a capacity of 105,000 fans, this daytime race with clear visibility of every overtaking move in Zandvoort delivers non-stop action. Insider Tips for Zandvoort

The banked Turn 3 grandstand is the most unique viewing experience in F1 - cars sweep through at steep angles. Wear orange to fit in with the Dutch fans. The beach is a 5-minute walk from the circuit - perfect for a pre-race swim. Dutch pancakes and stroopwafels from local vendors are unmissable. Book accommodation in Haarlem or Amsterdam.

How to Get There

Amsterdam Schiphol Airport is 30 minutes by train. Direct trains from Amsterdam Central to Zandvoort station run every 15 minutes on race days. The beach road is closed to cars during the event.
