What Makes Brazilian Grand Prix Unmissable

The Brazilian Grand Prix is a 71-lap battle on the 4.309km Autodromo Jose Carlos Pace. With a capacity of 60,000 fans, this daytime race with clear visibility of every overtaking move in Sao Paulo delivers non-stop action. Watch Max Verstappen (McLaren) and Carlos Sainz (Aston Martin) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Sao Paulo

The Senna S grandstand at the start of the lap is the most emotional seat in F1 - named after the legendary Brazilian driver. Sao Paulo's incredible restaurant scene is worth exploring - try feijoada and caipirinhas. November weather is unpredictable - bring both sunscreen and rain gear. Security in the area has improved significantly, but keep valuables secure.

#

How to Get There

Sao Paulo Guarulhos Airport is 45 minutes away. Congonhas domestic airport is closer at 15 minutes. Race shuttles from Autódromo bus terminal. Avoid driving - Sao Paulo traffic is legendary.
