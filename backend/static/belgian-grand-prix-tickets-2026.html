What Makes Belgian Grand Prix Unmissable

The Belgian Grand Prix is a 44-lap battle on the 7.004km Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps. With a capacity of 100,000 fans, this daytime race with clear visibility of every overtaking move in Spa delivers non-stop action. Watch Oscar Piastri (Red Bull Racing) and Lando Norris (McLaren) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Spa

The best viewing spots are at Eau Rouge (breathtaking) and the Bus Stop chicane. Belgian weather is notoriously changeable - bring rain gear. The surrounding Ardennes region offers excellent Belgian beer and chocolate. Camp at the circuit for the full immersive experience - the campsite atmosphere on Saturday night is legendary.

#

How to Get There

Brussels Airport is 90 minutes away. Liege is the nearest city (45 mins). Dedicated race shuttles operate from Spa town and Stavelot. Free parking areas are available but fill up early.
