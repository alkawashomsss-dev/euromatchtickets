What Makes Las Vegas Grand Prix Unmissable

The Las Vegas Grand Prix is a 50-lap battle on the 6.201km Las Vegas Strip Circuit. With a capacity of 105,000 fans, this spectacular night race under floodlights in Las Vegas delivers non-stop action. Insider Tips for Las Vegas

The Bellagio fountain grandstand is the most Instagram-worthy seat in F1. November nights in Vegas are cold (5-10C) - bring warm clothing despite the desert location. The Sphere is visible from multiple grandstands and displays F1 graphics during the race. Book a Strip-view hotel room for an incredible pre-race atmosphere. The after-parties in Vegas clubs are legendary.

How to Get There
