**How much are Arsenal FC tickets?**
Standard tickets start from €49. Premium seats from €129, VIP boxes from €399, and Executive Boxes from €1,499. Champions League matches are priced higher due to demand.

**Can I buy tickets for away matches?**
Yes, we have tickets for Arsenal FC away matches across all competitions. Away section tickets are limited and sell out quickly - book early.

**What is the best section for atmosphere?**
The most vocal supporters are typically in the home end/ultras section. For the best overall view, choose the main stand or premium midfield seats.

**How early should I arrive at Emirates Stadium?**
We recommend arriving at least 90 minutes before kick-off. For big matches (North London Derby derbies, Champions League), arrive 2 hours early to enjoy the pre-match atmosphere.

Yes, Emirates Stadium offers wheelchair-accessible seating, accessible toilets, and dedicated assistance. Contact us when booking to arrange accessible tickets.
