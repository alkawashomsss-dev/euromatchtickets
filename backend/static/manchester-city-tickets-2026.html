Frequently Asked Questions

**How much are Manchester City tickets?**
Standard tickets start from €49. Premium seats from €129, VIP boxes from €399, and Executive Boxes from €1,499. Champions League matches are priced higher due to demand.

**What matches can I see at Etihad Stadium?**
Manchester City plays approximately 25 home matches per season across Premier League, Champions League, and domestic cup competitions. Derby matches and Champions League knockout rounds are the highest demand.

**Is Etihad Stadium accessible for disabled fans?**
Yes, Etihad Stadium offers wheelchair-accessible seating, accessible toilets, and dedicated assistance. Contact us when booking to arrange accessible tickets.

**What is the best section for atmosphere?**
The most vocal supporters are typically in the home end/ultras section. For the best overall view, choose the main stand or premium midfield seats.

**Can I buy tickets for away matches?**
Yes, we have tickets for Manchester City away matches across all competitions. Away section tickets are limited and sell out quickly - book early.
