Insider Tips for Baku

The Turn 8 area through the Old City is the most unique section in F1 - cars thread through medieval walls. The main straight grandstand offers drama as cars accelerate to 350km/h. Baku's Old City restaurants and carpet shops are worth exploring on non-race days. The Flame Towers viewpoint gives a panoramic view of the circuit.
