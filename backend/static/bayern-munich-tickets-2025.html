Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Allianz Arena accessible for disabled fans?**
Yes, Allianz Arena offers wheelchair-accessible seating, accessible toilets, and dedicated assistance. Contact us when booking to arrange accessible tickets.

**Can I buy tickets for away matches?**
Yes, we have tickets for Bayern Munich away matches across all competitions. Away section tickets are limited and sell out quickly - book early.

**What matches can I see at Allianz Arena?**
Bayern Munich plays approximately 25 home matches per season across Bundesliga, Champions League, and domestic cup competitions. Derby matches and Champions League knockout rounds are the highest demand.

**How early should I arrive at Allianz Arena?**
We recommend arriving at least 90 minutes before kick-off. For big matches (Der Klassiker derbies, Champions League), arrive 2 hours early to enjoy the pre-match atmosphere.

**What is the best section for atmosphere?**
The most vocal supporters are typically in the home end/ultras section. For the best overall view, choose the main stand or premium midfield seats.
