What Makes Qatar Grand Prix Unmissable

The Qatar Grand Prix is a 57-lap battle on the 5.419km Lusail International Circuit. With a capacity of 40,000 fans, this spectacular night race under floodlights in Lusail delivers non-stop action. Watch Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) and Oscar Piastri (Red Bull Racing) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Lusail

The main grandstand offers excellent views of the start/finish and pit entry. Qatar's desert temperatures can be extreme during the day but comfortable at night for the race. Souq Waqif in Doha is a must-visit traditional market. The Pearl-Qatar island offers luxury dining and shopping.

#

How to Get There

Hamad International Airport in Doha is 25 minutes from the circuit. Dedicated race buses operate from Doha metro stations. Taxis and Uber are readily available.
