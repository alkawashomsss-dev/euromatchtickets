What Makes Chinese Grand Prix Unmissable

The Chinese Grand Prix is a 56-lap battle on the 5.451km Shanghai International Circuit. With a capacity of 200,000 fans, this daytime race with clear visibility of every overtaking move in Shanghai delivers non-stop action. Watch Fernando Alonso (Ferrari) and Lando Norris (McLaren) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Shanghai

The main grandstand is enormous and well-covered. Shanghai's efficient metro system reaches the circuit directly via Line 11. The food options inside the circuit include both international and authentic Chinese cuisine. Visit the paddock area on Thursday for driver autograph sessions.

#

How to Get There

Shanghai Pudong Airport connects to the circuit via metro in about 90 minutes. High-speed rail from Beijing takes just 4.5 hours. Circuit shuttle buses run from multiple metro stations.
