What Makes Saudi Arabian Grand Prix Unmissable

The Saudi Arabian Grand Prix is a 50-lap battle on the 6.174km Jeddah Corniche Circuit. With a capacity of 55,000 fans, this spectacular night race under floodlights in Jeddah delivers non-stop action. Watch Oscar Piastri (Mercedes) and Charles Leclerc (Red Bull Racing) push the limits of modern F1 machinery.

#

Insider Tips for Jeddah

The Turn 1 grandstand offers incredible views of braking duels. Evening temperatures are warm, so dress light. The concert area near the circuit hosts world-class performers on race weekend. VIP packages include access to the Jeddah Yacht Club with sea views.

#

How to Get There

King Abdulaziz International Airport is 20 minutes away. Dedicated race shuttles operate from major hotels. Ride-sharing apps work well in Jeddah.
